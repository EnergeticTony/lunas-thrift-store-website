# Luna's Thrift Store Website

Luna's Thrift Store currently operates through in-person engagement, social media outreach, volunteer communication, and institutional knowledge shared between Executive Committee members. While these methods have supported the growth of the initiative, there is currently no centralized digital platform, outside of our social media accounts and LinkTree, that serves as the public-facing home for the thrift store and clothing drive initiatives.

The Luna's Thrift Store Website aims to serve as the digital hub for the initiative by providing information about the store, promoting events and partnerships, supporting volunteer recruitment, documenting impact, and preserving organizational history. Rather than functioning solely as a marketing website, the platform seeks to strengthen Luna's identity as a sustainability and basic needs resource while improving accessibility for students, volunteers, campus partners, and community members.

The website will act as a bridge between operations and outreach by centralizing important information that is currently distributed across Instagram posts, flyers, Discord messages, and institutional memory. Through this platform, students will be able to learn about the mission of the thrift store, discover upcoming events, explore volunteer opportunities, and understand the impact of clothing recovery efforts on campus.

Additionally, the website will support the long-term sustainability of the initiative by serving as a repository for bylaw changes, annual reports, strategic plans, transition materials, and historical milestones. In doing so, the website contributes to Luna's broader goal of institutionalization and long-term organizational stability.

## How it Works

The Luna's Thrift Store Website operates as an informational and engagement platform for students, volunteers, staff, and community partners.

Visitors arriving on the website are introduced to Luna's mission, current initiatives, and recent impact through a modern landing page featuring photographs, statistics, and recent updates.

Users can navigate to dedicated pages including:
- Home
- About Us
- Our Mission, Vision, and Values
- Visit the Store - Hours and Location
- Purple Bin Clothing Drive Information
- Volunteer Opportunities
- Events and Programs
- Partnerships and Collaborations
- Impact and Annual Reports
- Strategic Plans
- Executive Committee and Leadership Team
- Contact Information

Potential volunteers can complete an interest form and learn more about the various teams within the organization, including Events & Outreach, Media, and Operations. Campus departments and external organizations can use collaboration forms to propose partnerships, request thrift swaps, or coordinate sustainability initiatives.

The website may also feature an impact dashboard displaying organizational metrics such as volunteer participation, event attendance, clothing recovery estimates, and community engagement statistics. Through these features, the website functions as both a public-facing marketing platform and an institutional knowledge repository for future leaders.
